Banks and apps Xtracto recognises

Xtracto reads the alerts these applications send to your phone and turns them into transactions. The list grows with the formats people send in.

The list

Caja Rural · Ruralvíacom.rsi.nbaCard payment, Direct debit

It also recognises the alert that a charge has been cancelled, to strike through the movement already recorded.

Recognising an alert is not connecting to a bank. Xtracto asks for no credentials, does not use Open Banking and cannot read your real balance: it only reads notifications you already receive.

Yours is not there?

There are two different situations, and they are not fixed the same way.

Your bank does not appear in Settings

Then Xtracto is not reading anything from that app. It can only read notifications from the apps in its catalogue, and if yours is not in it, not a single alert is stored: there is nothing to send us yet. Ask us for it and we will add it — the bank's name is enough.

Request my bank

Your bank does appear, but its alerts don't become expenses

That means the app stores the alert but does not understand its format yet: you will see it in the Captures tab as "Unparsed". From there you can send us the message template without sending us any of your data: before anything is shared, it replaces amounts, dates, merchants and numbers with placeholders and shows you exactly what will go out.
