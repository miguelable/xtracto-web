Request your bank

Xtracto can only read alerts from the apps in its catalogue, and no catalogue covers the thousands of banks out there. If yours is not on the list, tell us which one it is and we will add it.

The name is enough. You don't need to know anything technical, sign up for anything, or give us any detail about you: no email, no name, no phone.

The name as the app is called on your phone.

We need it because different countries have banks with the same name, so this is how we find the right app and not another one.

If you know it, you save us a step. It appears in the app's Google Play page address, right after id=. If you don't know it, leave it empty.

Sending it publishes a public issue in the project's repository, with the bank's name and country. Your email address is not stored, nor any identifier of yours; your IP is used only to rate-limit submissions per hour and is stored hashed, never in the clear. See the privacy policy.
